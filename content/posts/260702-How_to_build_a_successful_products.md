## Step 1. Find a problem to solve.
- What annoys me? What is hard for people? What wastes their time?
- helps people easier, faster, or cheaper.

## step2. A simple solution
- Don't try to make something huge and perfect right away.
- What is the most basic version that works?

## Step 3: Talk to real people
- Show idea to friends, family, or strangers.
Ask: Would you use this? What don't you like? What would make it better?
Listen carefully — their feedback is gold.

## Step 4: Build it (cheaply and quickly)
Don't spend too much money at the start.
Use free tools if you can.
Make one small version, test it, fix it, test it again — repeat.

## Step 5: Find your first customers
Who needs this the most? Go find them!

Tell people about it clearly: What is it? Why is it good? How much does it cost?

Start with 10 or 20 customers — not 1,000.

## Step 6: Keep improving
Listen to what customers say.

Fix problems, add new features, make it better little by little.

A successful product is never "finished" — it always grows.
